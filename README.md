# Creator Fees

Welcome.

This page is for the creator of the featured GROK-related tokens.

## How it works

Creator fees are generated from trading activity on Pump.fun.

If you are the designated creator/fee recipient, you can claim the available fees associated with your tokens directly through Pump.fun.

## Claim your fees

1. Open Pump.fun.
2. Connect the wallet associated with the creator account.
3. Open your creator fees / earnings.
4. Review the available balance.
5. Claim the available fees.

## Important

* Only connect a wallet that you control.
* Never share your seed phrase or private key.
* Always verify the token and wallet details before signing a transaction.
* Creator fees depend on actual trading activity and are not guaranteed.

This repository is only an informational page. Fee collection and distribution are handled by Pump.fun.
